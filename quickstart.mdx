This guide takes you from zero to your first transaction with Itaja. Itaja is
in private beta, so the first step is getting access.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, you need:

- An Itaja account — granted during beta onboarding (see step 1)
- Sandbox credentials for a payment provider you want to test with
- A backend or serverless function able to receive webhooks

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request access">
    Join the waitlist on [getitaja.com](https://www.getitaja.com). Once your
    team is onboarded, you receive access to your dashboard and your API
    credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a payment provider">
    In your Itaja dashboard, add the sandbox credentials of the provider you
    want to test with. You can connect several providers and let Itaja route
    between them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first payment">
    Create a payment from your dashboard or through the API. You get a hosted
    payment link you can send to a test customer — no frontend work required
    for the first transaction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the webhook">
    Itaja notifies your backend on every payment event. Point a webhook
    endpoint at your backend, verify the event signature, and confirm the
    payment is marked as settled in your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    When your integration is verified end to end, switch your provider
    credentials from sandbox to production in the dashboard, and complete the
    merchant-of-record onboarding for your business.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Questions during onboarding? Reach out through
  [getitaja.com](https://www.getitaja.com) — we answer every beta request
  personally.
</Tip>
