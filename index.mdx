Itaja is the **merchant of record** and monetization infrastructure for African
SaaS. One integration to **collect** payments, **bill** customers, manage
**subscriptions**, and **payout** across Africa — through the mobile money and
card providers your customers already use.

<Note>
  Itaja is in private beta. Access is granted progressively — join the
  waitlist on [getitaja.com](https://www.getitaja.com) and we onboard your
  team.
</Note>

## What Itaja handles

- **Collections.** Mobile money and card payments through our payment service
  providers — Kkiapay, FedaPay, and PawaPay at launch — with one integration
  instead of one per PSP and per country.
- **Billing and subscriptions.** Invoicing, recurring charges, dunning, and
  credits, so you don't rebuild a billing engine for each market.
- **Payouts.** Merchant payouts and reversals, in the currency and rail that
  fits each market.
- **Merchant of record.** We sit between you and the payment rails, so
  compliance, reconciliation, and money movement aren't your problem.
