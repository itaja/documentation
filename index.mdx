Itaja is the **Merchant of Record** for African SaaS and AI products that sell
globally. Collections, subscriptions, invoicing, tax compliance, disputes, and
payouts — all handled. You focus on your product.

We are the legal seller of record. Compliance, taxes, and disputes are in our
name, so selling across borders never means filing paperwork in a country
you've never visited.

<Note>
  Itaja is in private beta. Access is granted progressively — join the
  waitlist on [getitaja.com](https://www.getitaja.com) and we onboard your
  team.
</Note>

## Why Itaja

<Columns>
  <Column>
    ### One integration, one contract

    **The problem.** Selling across African markets usually means one contract
    per payment provider and per country, then reconciling everything by hand.

    **Itaja's answer.** One integration and one contract cover mobile money in
    Africa and cards and wallets worldwide. You don't hold relationships with
    payment providers — we do.
  </Column>
  <Column>
    ### Compliance is not your job

    **The problem.** Every country you sell into brings its own tax and
    regulatory obligations. Most teams either ignore them or drown in them.

    **Itaja's answer.** As your Merchant of Record, we calculate, collect, and
    remit taxes in each country where you sell. No filing on your side. This
    isn't an add-on — it's how Itaja works by default.
  </Column>
  <Column>
    ### One rate, nothing hidden

    **The problem.** Processor pricing stacks up: conversion surcharges,
    recurring-billing fees, card-type supplements, payout fees.

    **Itaja's answer.** One flat rate — **8% + 100 FCFA per transaction**.
    Nothing is added based on country, payment method, or subscription. The
    rate you see is the total price. See [Pricing](/merchant-of-record/pricing).
  </Column>
</Columns>

## Quick start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get access">
    Join the waitlist on [getitaja.com](https://www.getitaja.com). We onboard
    beta teams progressively.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your credentials">
    Once onboarded, you receive your dashboard access and API credentials for
    the sandbox environment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first payment">
    Create a payment link from your dashboard or through the API — no frontend
    work needed for a first transaction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your webhook">
    Point a webhook endpoint at your backend and verify the event signature, so
    your app stays in sync with every payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Switch from sandbox to production and complete your Merchant of Record
    onboarding. See the [Quickstart](/quickstart).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How fast can I start accepting payments?">
    Once onboarded, most teams go from zero to a first test payment in minutes:
    create a payment, share the link. Going live depends on completing your
    Merchant of Record onboarding.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What payment methods can my customers use?">
    Bank cards and crypto are supported in every covered country. Mobile money
    coverage varies by country and operator — check the live coverage table on
    [getitaja.com](https://www.getitaja.com).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How does pricing work?">
    One flat rate of 8% + 100 FCFA per successful transaction. The 100 FCFA
    fixed fee is always borne by the end customer; the 8% is borne by you or
    passed to the customer — your choice. Nothing is added based on country,
    payment method, or subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is Itaja different from a payment processor?">
    A payment processor moves money — tax compliance, filings, and disputes
    remain yours. Itaja is a Merchant of Record: we are the legal seller, so
    tax, compliance, and disputes are handled in our name, and you don't need
    your own payment license or local entity to sell.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who is Itaja for?">
    African SaaS and AI creators who sell to customers in Africa and worldwide
    and don't want to become payment, tax, and compliance experts to do it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
