Itaja is not a checkout button. It's everything that happens after the click
on **pay**: orchestration between payment providers, billing, tax, and getting
your money back to you.

## The lifecycle of a payment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your customer pays">
    They check out through a hosted payment link or a flow you built on the
    API. They pay with the methods available in their country — mobile money,
    card, or wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Itaja collects as the seller of record">
    The collection is operated by licensed payment providers. We hold the
    contractual relationships with them — that's the separation that lets you
    collect without your own payment license.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Taxes are handled at the source">
    VAT and local obligations in the customer's country are calculated,
    collected, and remitted. Nothing to file on your side.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your share is settled">
    Your commission — the price minus the Itaja rate — is recorded in your
    dashboard, transaction by transaction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You get paid out">
    Your balance is paid out to your local account on your payout schedule. No
    entity to create in each market.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What stays yours

You keep your product, your pricing, your brand, and your customer
relationship. What moves to Itaja is the legal and financial machinery of the
sale — the part that was never your differentiator.
