When Itaja is your Merchant of Record, the legal side of every sale moves to
us. This page lists what that covers — and what it deliberately doesn't.

## What Itaja takes on

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Tax calculation" icon="calculator">
    VAT and local tax obligations in the customer's country are calculated at
    checkout, on every transaction.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tax collection and remittance" icon="landmark">
    Taxes are collected with the payment and remitted where they are due. You
    never touch a tax form.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Disputes and chargebacks" icon="shield">
    Payment disputes are handled in our name, as the seller of record — not
    yours.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Provider relationships" icon="file-contract">
    The contracts with licensed payment providers are ours. You don't hold a
    payment license and don't need one to sell.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What stays yours

- Your product and its legality — what you sell must be lawful in the markets
  you sell into.
- Your customers — the relationship, the support, the product experience.
- Your own corporate obligations in the country where your business is based.

<Note>
  Coverage follows our licensed providers and the applicable regulation in
  each market. The exact scope for your business is confirmed during Merchant
  of Record onboarding.
</Note>
