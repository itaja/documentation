A **Merchant of Record (MoR)** is the legal seller of a transaction. When a
customer pays, the MoR is the party whose name is on the sale: it is
responsible for tax calculation, collection, and remittance, for regulatory
compliance in each market, and for disputes and refunds.

If you sell software across borders without an MoR, all of that is yours: one
set of obligations per country, per tax regime, per payment method.

With an MoR, none of it is.

## MoR, processor, or gateway?

These three roles are often confused. They sit at different depths of the same
transaction.

| | Gateway | Payment processor | Merchant of Record |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Moves money** | No | Yes | Yes |
| **Legal seller of record** | No | No | Yes |
| **Tax calculation, filing, remittance** | No | No | Yes |
| **Disputes and chargebacks** | No | No | Yes |
| **You need a local entity or license** | Yes | Yes | No |

A gateway connects you to providers. A processor moves money. Only a Merchant
of Record takes on the legal side of the sale — that's the layer Itaja
operates.

## Who we are

Itaja is a Merchant of Record and monetization infrastructure built for
African SaaS and AI creators. The service is operated by **Lixali Studio,
LLC**, incorporated in Delaware, USA, by a team based in West Africa.

We exist because of an asymmetry we kept hitting ourselves: African builders
can reach customers anywhere, but the plumbing to get paid across African
markets — multiple providers, one contract each, manual reconciliation, local
compliance — was built for enterprises, not for product teams.

Itaja compresses that plumbing into one integration and one contract:

- **Collections** through the payment methods your customers actually use —
  mobile money in Africa, cards and wallets worldwide.
- **Billing** — subscriptions, invoicing, credits, and license keys.
- **Compliance** — taxes and local obligations calculated, collected, and
  remitted where you sell.
- **Payouts** — your share settled and paid out to your local account.

Collections are operated by licensed payment providers with whom we hold the
contractual relationships. That separation is exactly what lets you collect
payments without holding a payment license or creating an entity in every
market you sell into.

## Do you need an MoR?

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="An MoR is a good fit if…" icon="check">
    - You sell to customers in multiple countries
    - You don't have (or don't want) a local entity in each market
    - You'd rather not become a tax and compliance expert
    - You want disputes and refunds handled for you
  </Card>
  <Card title="You may not need one if…" icon="minus">
    - You sell only in your home country
    - You already have entities and filings covered
    - You genuinely want to operate the payment side yourself
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
