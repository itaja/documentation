Itaja charges one flat rate: **8% + 100 FCFA per successful transaction**.

The rate is the total price. Nothing is added to it — not based on the
country, not based on the payment method, not on subscriptions, not on
payouts.

## Who bears what

| Component | Borne by |
|---|---|
| 100 FCFA fixed fee | Always the end customer |
| 8% variable fee | You or the customer — your choice |

When you pass the 8% to the customer, your price is what lands on your
account. When you absorb it, your price minus 8%.

## What the commission covers

- Payment collection, everywhere you sell
- Taxes and compliance in each country where you sell
- Billing — subscriptions, credits, invoices, license keys
- Payouts to your account

## Other currencies

The model is denominated in CFA francs (XOF). For sales in other markets, the
commission is converted at the day's rate.
